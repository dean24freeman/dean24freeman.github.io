Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026.

This policy covers "rclone home backup", a personal app used only by its owner, Dean Freeman.

What the app accesses

The app uses Google Drive access to upload backup copies of the owner's own files to the owner's own Google Drive.

What is collected and shared

No personal data is collected, stored, sold or shared with anyone.

The Google sign-in token stays on the owner's home server. It is never sent anywhere except to Google.

No other people use this app, and it does not access other people's data.

Google API data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from your Google Account, under Security, Third-party apps and services.

Contact

Questions can be raised through the owner's GitHub profile: github.com/dean24freeman.
